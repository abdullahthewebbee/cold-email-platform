ABOUT

Cold email, built to reach people.

DevOutreach is a product of Devlet Technology — built for teams who take deliverability seriously and want full control over their outreach.

Our mission

Cold email is one of the highest-ROI channels in B2B — but most tools optimize for send volume, not for actually landing in the inbox. DevOutreach was built the other way around. Deliverability comes first: DNS checks, health scoring, content risk analysis, and guardrails are core features, not upsells.

We believe you should own your sending. That's why DevOutreach connects to your own Gmail, Office 365, and SMTP accounts through official APIs — never shared infrastructure that puts your reputation at risk.

Who it's for

DevOutreach serves solo founders sending their first campaigns, growth teams scaling outbound, and agencies running outreach for multiple clients with full white-label and multi-tenant isolation.

Built by Devlet Technology — a software studio focused on practical, high-quality tools for modern businesses. Reach us at hello@devlet.tech.
