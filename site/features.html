Sequences

Branch-tree conditional sequences

Design flows that adapt to each lead. Steps define content; the branch tree defines the logic and timing — including instant follow-ups when a lead opens but doesn't reply.

  • Conditions: opened, not opened, opened-no-reply, clicked, replied
  • Wait days plus minute/hour-level instant triggers
  • A/B variants on any step
  • Visual drag-and-drop canvas with zoom and minimap
Deliverability

A deliverability suite, not an afterthought

DevOutreach bundles the tools that keep you in the inbox — most platforms make you buy these separately or skip them entirely.

  • DNS Doctor: SPF, DKIM, DMARC, blacklist, MTA-STS, BIMI
  • 0-100 Deliverability Health Score per domain
  • Spam Score pre-check live in the editor
  • Send Autopsy: auth snapshot and content risk per email
  • Complaint Rate Guardrail auto-pauses risky campaigns
Extension
